Ramp Router

Ramp · Jun–Sep 2026 · router.com

Router is Ramp's LLM gateway: one endpoint for OpenAI, Anthropic, and open models, with every request routed to the lowest-cost model that meets its performance needs. It cuts inference costs ~40% on average, with automatic fallbacks, request-level observability, and built-in privacy controls.

I was an early engineer on the team. I helped design and build the initial gateway, onboarding, and attribution, and wrote the first intelligent routing strategies — routing based on the semantic difficulty of each request rather than static rules.

Over the same summer I co-ran BugBasher, an experiment in letting an AI agent run a business with a Ramp card.
